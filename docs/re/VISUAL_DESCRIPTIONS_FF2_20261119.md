# FFII enemy art audit

The art IDs refer to the installed `monster_asset` master. The local contact
sheets under `../tools/visual_audit_20261119` show the exact variants. For each
row, combine the visible form and the named variant details. The Toad sprite
is included as its own rendered form. This wording is awaiting localization
and in-game speech integration.

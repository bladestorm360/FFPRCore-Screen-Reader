# FFII field and battle appearance audit

These descriptions refer to exact installed art keys. Native character
status and field and battle art tables supply the party assignments.
`ff2-field-costumes.json` and `ff2-battle-costumes.json` in the local visual
audit folder index the bundles and reviewed frames. Wording awaits
localization and integration.

# FFIII field and battle appearance audit

These descriptions use exact installed art keys. The same sprite can be
assigned to multiple actors. The local `ff3-field-costumes.json` and
`ff3-battle-costumes.json` record the art tables, bundles and reviewed
frames. Wording awaits localization and integration.
